## Interpretation and implementation cautions

- The student's funds figure is for the study title and is a means-of-support proof, not tuition and not an all-in relocation budget. Do not reuse it as the graduate job-search amount without an additional source.
- Student work allowance and post-study work rights are distinct: §16b work accounting while studying versus any occupation under the separate §20 graduate job-search title.
- Successful graduation does not grant the §20 title automatically. The graduate applies, shows completion and coverage/support, and needs a timely new title.
- A job offer does not alone confer §18b or Blue Card status. Evaluate the actual credential, offer, job level/occupation, salary, licensing and approvals.
- A German degree's special §18c path begins only after employment on a listed skilled-worker title; the study period is not interchangeable with the two-year employment title period or 24-month contribution record. General §9 study residence credits are half for its residence-period rule.
- Blue Card settlement (21 months with B1 / 27 with A1) is an alternative statutory track. Do not add it to or combine it with the German-graduate two-year track.
- Permanent settlement is not naturalisation. Keep passport/citizenship projections out until nationality law is researched independently.
- None of these research sources resolves a named master's programme, consular post, individual insurance arrangement, or local foreigners authority checklist. Unknown amounts stay unknown; tuition and living costs vary by institution/state/applicant.
