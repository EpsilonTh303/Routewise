# Post-study Europe route evidence — 25 October 2026

Directly retrieved official issuing-authority pages for two post-study routes. Structured route and claim records are in [poststudy-europe-2026-10-25.json](poststudy-europe-2026-10-25.json). Checked 2026-10-25 14:37 UTC. This is a source review for downstream design, not legal advice, an eligibility decision, or a complete study-to-settlement audit.

## Netherlands — orientation year

Primary route page: [IND orientation year](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/residence-permit-for-orientation-year), last updated 7 October 2026. It gives a three-year application window and enumerates eligible Dutch degrees/programmes, Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters, designated foreign master's/doctoral/post-master's programmes and certain research. A foreign institution must meet the IND top-200 test in at least two of QS, THE and ShanghaiRanking at the graduation date; course language/accepted language evidence and Nuffic diploma evaluation also matter. This is not an open permit for every foreign or Dutch graduate. The page says no income proof is needed for this application, validity is one year, it cannot be extended, and work/self-employment is unrestricted. The displayed fee is €254; the official [fees page](https://ind.nl/en/fees-costs-of-an-application) was checked for that fee.

For employment after this permit, the graduate must switch to a distinct status. The [IND highly skilled migrant page](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant) requires an employment contract, an IND-recognised sponsor, and market-rate salary. The official [2026 salary table](https://ind.nl/en/required-amounts-income-requirements) lists €3,122/month gross excluding holiday pay for the reduced criterion applicable to eligible orientation-year graduates. It is not a universal wage requirement for every work route.

The [IND permanent residence page](https://ind.nl/en/replace-extend-renew-and-change/permanent-residency/permanent-residence-permit) states general criteria including five consecutive years of valid residence, main residence, timely extensions, current non-temporary purpose, income, registration and civic integration. Some exceptions exist. This packet does not determine whether a particular permit history qualifies or project an application date.

## Finland — look for work/start a business

Primary route page: [Migri post-study/research job-seeking permit](https://migri.fi/en/application-for-students-and-researchers-residence-permit-to-look-for-work). Eligibility depends on a prior study/research residence permit and completion in Finland; national research permit cases are narrower than EU Directive research cases. The page states a five-year deadline after the prior permit expired, up to two years' validity (up to three parts, each at least six months, with the last ending within three years of the first start), €800/month support (€19,200 available for two years), and fees of €750 online/€800 paper for first permits or €230 online/€430 paper for extensions. It grants unrestricted work. First permits are filed abroad; extended permits in Finland. A timely extended application can carry interim work rights under the stated conditions.

If the graduate has a qualifying job or business, Migri describes the separate [degree completed in Finland permit](https://migri.fi/en/degree-completed-in-finland1); this review only records its high-level relationship to the route. The post-study permit itself can carry unrestricted work; it does not guarantee permanent residence.

Finland's PR law changed on **8 January 2026**. The [Migri change notice](https://migri.fi/en/-/amendments-to-provisions-on-permanent-residence-permits-enter-into-force-on-8-january-2026) lists distinct paths, including six years' continuous A-permit residence with two years' Finnish work and B1 language; four years and over €40,000 annual income; and a Finnish higher-education degree path with A2/developing Finnish or Swedish plus continued A-permit and other requirements. See the detailed [permanent-residence application page](https://migri.fi/en/application-for-a-permanent-residence-permit) for qualification scope and other conditions. Do not reuse pre-2026 generic four-year PR assumptions.

## Limits and implementation cautions

- This evidence covers post-study stages. Admission, the student permit, tuition, study-time work rules, insurance and dependant costs still need separate official research.
- Amounts are native EUR. The Finnish €800/month and €19,200 are means-of-support proof thresholds, not a cost-of-living estimate. Fees are kept separate. The Dutch €3,122/month is an employment salary criterion, not funds to show.
- Avoid an all-in relocation/study-year figure: tuition and dependant needs are institution-, programme- and profile-specific and not covered here.
- Preserve graduation date, institution/credential, prior permit class/dates, filing date/location, foreign rankings and evaluation, language evidence, work offer/sponsor/salary, and full residence chronology as profile facts.
- A post-study permit, job, PR or citizenship are separate decisions. No route guarantees settlement. Citizenship was not researched.
