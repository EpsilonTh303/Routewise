# TK student health/care tariffs — selected source review

Checked 2026-10-26 at 12:25:15 UTC. This verifies selected tariff claims, not applicant insurance eligibility or the whole immigration route.

- Tariff source: https://www.tk.de/en/contribution-foreign-students-german-health-insurance-2038022 — directly retrieved. Page date 2026-02-04; rates effective 2026-01-01. Monthly health plus long-term care totals: EUR 141.16 for childless students below 23 and the one-child band; EUR 146.29 for the childless surcharge band from 23. Multiple-child discounts are outside these two records. End date is not supplied.
- Cross-check: https://www.tk.de/techniker/versicherung/gut-versichert-in-jeder-lebenslage/versichert-als-studierende/beitrag-studierende-2131472 — directly indexed official TK tariff page, dated 2025-12-22, same effective date and totals.
- Eligibility source: https://www.tk.de/resource/blob/2195380/1b1c09f21c6c695da2fcde7741a0a750/beratungsblatt-krankenversichert-im-studium-2026-englisch-data.pdf — directly retrieved, pages 1–2. Requires qualifying enrollment and student insurance status; free family coverage, priority insurance/foreign entitlements, full-time self-employment and excluded study statuses need consideration. The student tariff normally ends by the end of the semester in which the student turns 30; continuation requires case review. Age alone cannot establish applicability.

Implementation conservatively requires dated TK student-tariff and care-band confirmation and an explicit family-insurance answer. At 30+ a current-semester/exception review is also required. Unresolved age-23 care-band transitions, missing information, multiple-child cases and dates outside the reviewed 2026 edition return no price. Future-year blocking is a review boundary, not an asserted legal expiration on 31 December.

Each record is one combined monthly expense, not two additive alternatives. Neither is financing proof. No full study coverage duration or total budget is inferred.
