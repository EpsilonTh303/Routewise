# Asia-Pacific, Gulf, Middle East, Caucasus and Balkans source notes

Checked 2026-10-25. This is a claim-level audit record, not whole-route verification. Route status reports whether the route appears to exist/open in the retrieved source, not whether an applicant qualifies. Where evidence came from the prior project review rather than a page retrieved during this pass, the JSON labels it `official-index` and says it was inherited.

## Freshly retrieved official material and short locators

- **Hong Kong TTPS** — Immigration Department FAQ, “Eligibility Criteria”: Category A prior-year HKD 2.5m; Categories B/C depend on eligible university graduate criteria; “initial stay of 36 months (Category A) / 24 months (Categories B and C).” [Official TTPS page](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/services/visas/TTPS.html)
- **Singapore EP** — MOM eligibility page, “EP qualifying salary (Stage 1)” and “COMPASS (Stage 2)”: starts SGD 5,600 per month outside financial services; age/sector adjusted, COMPASS ordinarily 40 points. “Key facts” says first-time up to 2 years, renewals up to 3 years. [MOM eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility) [MOM key facts](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/key-facts)
- **Malaysia MM2H Silver** — MOTAC page, “Financial Requirements”: “Fixed Deposit (FD) of USD150,000”; “Compulsory to purchase and own a residence”; minimum RM600,000; 5-year term in category overview; age 25+, 90 days/year if below age 50. [Silver category](https://www.mm2h.gov.my/category/silver) [Category overview](https://www.mm2h.gov.my/category/overview)
- **Taiwan Employment Gold Card** — official portal “What is the Taiwan Employment Gold Card?”: 1–3 years; no sponsorship/job precondition; open work rights; fees USD 100–310; PR described after three years. Portal summary says last changed 2020, so PR detail is not treated as current eligibility confirmation. [Gold Card overview](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/about/)
- **Thailand DTV** — Thai Embassy Washington page updated 2026-09-10: five-year validity and evidence of at least THB 500,000; Thai government overview lists qualifying workcation/soft power purposes. [Embassy DTV page](https://washingtondc.thaiembassy.org/en/page/dtv-visa)
- **Türkiye citizenship by investment** — NVI official citizenship FAQ: real estate USD 400,000 plus a three-year no-sale annotation; alternatives include USD 500,000 fixed capital, USD 500,000 bank deposit held three years, or 50 jobs. [NVI FAQ](https://www.nvi.gov.tr/sss-vatandaslik-hizmetleri)
- **Georgia entrepreneurial/work residence** — the assigned record is the work-residence branch: the Public Service Development Agency says entrepreneurial activity or registered labour immigration can qualify; legal monthly income/remuneration must reach five times the consumer subsistence minimum, while the enterprise must show GEL 50,000 annual turnover per applicant (GEL 35,000 for education/medical). The separate USD 300,000 investment residence threshold is not applicable to this record and was removed. [SDA residence permits](https://sda.gov.ge/en/products/migration-residence-permits/)
- **Montenegro property residence** — Government page says property ownership/disposal is a temporary residence basis; general permit validity is up to one year with means, accommodation and insurance conditions. A separate 2025 notice describes EUR 200,000 as a draft amendment proposal, not proven enacted law. [Temporary residence](https://www.gov.me/en/article/temporary-residence) [2025 draft notice](https://www.gov.me/en/article/government-adopts-amendments-to-the-law-on-foreigners-new-measures-to-regulate-residence)
- **Japan Business Manager** — Immigration Services Agency page says 2025 amendment effective 16 October 2025; official FAQ clarifies JPY 30m means business assets deployed, not always a corporation’s registered capital. [Amendment/FAQ](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/10_00237.html)
- **Japan HSP** — official PR guidance: 70 points maintained for 3 years or 80 points for 1 year may qualify for shortened PR residence, subject to general criteria. [PR guidelines](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/nyukan_nyukan50.html)
- **New Zealand SMC** — current INZ visa and operational manual: six points from qualification, registration or income plus eligible NZ work experience where needed; job/job offer and general health, character and English requirements apply. Wage threshold is hourly, so omitted from structured `financialRequirements` until the schema accepts an hourly period. [SMC visa](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa/) [SMC manual](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/opsmanual/90254.htm)
- **Korea F-2-7** — official KIS homepage was retrieved and linked to the Visa Portal. The portal page rendered an interactive navigator without route-specific F-2-7 criteria; no numeric claims are asserted. [KIS](https://immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do) [Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10101)

## Retried blocked/indexed sources

- **Saudi Premium Residency** — both direct product and regulations URLs redirected to a JavaScript/logout shell. Official-domain search index returned the current fee table: limited SAR 100,000/year; unlimited SAR 800,000; limited duration 1–5 years renewable. Exact rows are listed in the Saudi regulations fee table and product-details page. These are program fees, not income or investment minimums. [Product details](https://pr.gov.sa/product-details-limited-unlimited) [Regulations](https://pr.gov.sa/premium-residency-regulations)
- **Indonesia E33G** — direct national E33G URL returned 403. Freshly retrieved official Bontang immigration office page identifies E33G special requirements: USD 60,000 annual income and overseas employer contract; a separate three-month statement shows USD 2,000 living funds. The audit uses the Bontang page as the source for both amounts and does not claim unverified Rp7m checkout fees. [National E33G URL](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33G) [Bontang immigration page](https://bontang.imigrasi.go.id/layanan-publik/kategori/wna/sub/informasi-visa-republik-indonesia)
- **Indonesia Second Home** — freshly retrieved Jakarta Barat Immigration checklist distinguishes USD 130,000 state-bank savings from USD 1,000,000 Indonesian apartment/property title. The Yogyakarta official news item confirms proof-of-funds and 90-day reporting, but gives no numeric threshold and is not used as the source for the USD 1m amount. [Jakarta Barat checklist](https://jakartabarat.imigrasi.go.id/pelaporan-proof-of-fund-2/) [Yogyakarta immigration news](https://jogja.imigrasi.go.id/warga-negara-asing-bisa-ajukan-second-home-visa-tanpa-penjamin/)
- **Thailand LTR** — fresh official BOI search results returned its 2025 brochure text: Wealthy Global Citizens have no income floor, USD 1m total personal assets including Thai investment and USD 500k qualifying Thai investment; Work-from-Thailand Professionals: USD 80k/year over the past two years, or USD 40k/year over the past two years plus specified credentials/IP/Series A, and qualifying employer conditions. The brochure wording does not establish an averaged annual-income calculation. Result is indexed PDF content, not page-open evidence. [BOI 2025 brochure](https://ltr.boi.go.th/documents/BOI-brochure%202025-LTR.pdf)
