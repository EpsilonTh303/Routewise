The primary agent owns acceptance, application, validation and commits. Research files are leads, not automatic catalog updates or whole-route verification.

## Corrections made during primary review

- Azerbaijan is on the US State Department's E-2 treaty-country list. A research inference that it was absent was rejected. Nationality eligibility alone does not establish E-2 investment/business eligibility.
- The official DV issuance page contains an updated statement and an inconsistent older body. No unqualified current pause/open conclusion is accepted.
- Canada's official Start-Up Visa page says new intake is paused from 30 June 2026; accepted applications continue processing. This is an availability issue, not an applicant's financial shortfall.
- Georgia's USD 300,000 investment residence evidence was removed from the entrepreneurial/work-residence route. Enterprise turnover and personal income are distinct.
- The Indonesia C7A URL was rejected as evidence for E33G. A retrieved official Bontang E33G description is cited with the national-page retrieval gap retained. Unconfirmed fee figures were removed. Second Home local reporting evidence is not generalized into a complete national application rule.
- An hourly NZ wage was not encoded as annual income. Argentina's five-SMVM and Colombia's three-SMLMV formulas are not five ARS or three COP.
- Egypt's project investment, separate nonrefundable contribution and contingent retention deposit are separate obligations, not one fungible asset amount. Native evidence remains descriptive pending complete rule modeling.
- Stale Grenada USD 150,000 evidence was superseded by current Investment Migration Agency sources. Mexico's consular examples remain post-specific. Canada's published funds table retains its visible July 2025 date even though retrieved in October 2026.
- Malformed UK and mismatched Finnish source URLs were corrected. Spain's Washington consular monthly formula is preserved with its annualization limitation, rather than silently choosing a different calculation.

## Acceptance and application policy

The immutable accepted batch is `official-catalog-audit-2026-10-25-v2`, in `src/lib/data/catalog-source-audit.ts`. It records claims, currencies, retrieval limits, availability and unknown fields separately. The original `official-review-2026-10-25-v1` and pathway lineage stay unchanged.

Unsupported archive USD gates, total year-one/dependent costs, generic processing and residence clocks are cleared to null. Only separately supported branch flags and direct-citizenship/PR-at-grant classifications are retained. Native monetary evidence is not silently converted to USD or summed into relocation spending. This deliberately leaves incomplete routes outside primary plans; it does not assert that legal routes do not exist.

Apply only after backup, migration, a conflict-free plan and isolated tests. Exact expected hashes and an immutable batch receipt protect custom edits and reject changed manifests. Atomic application preserves sources, revisions, inactive parents, saved profiles and prior evaluations. Application status and final validation are recorded in HANDOFF.md, not inferred from this research document.

New study/post-study routes are a later catalog phase. STUDY_PATHWAYS.md describes conditional transitions and official starting points; it adds no route or automatic residence promise.
