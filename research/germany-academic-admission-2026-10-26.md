# Germany programme-specific admission pilot — Bonn Computer Science MSc

Checked 2026-10-26 by direct web retrieval of official University of Bonn pages. This is a narrow academic-admission example for one named programme and current intake, separate from student-visa, post-study-work, or settlement eligibility. It is a source-backed checklist, not an admission decision. Foreign qualification equivalence, subject matching, transcript categorisation, language-proof acceptance, scoring, and an offer must remain manual/unknown until the University of Bonn Examination Board decides them.

## Selected programme and source set

**Programme:** University of Bonn, consecutive Master of Science in Computer Science (single-subject, taught in English), 120 ECTS, normally four semesters full-time. This report covers Computer Science only; Bonn's Artificial Intelligence and Cyber Security programmes have different criteria and are not included.

## Machine-implementable checklist for this programme

These are **individual documentary checks**. A supplied positive fact can mean only “applicant reports evidence”; no evidence is not a pass. There is no automatic comparison of an Azerbaijani/foreign transcript to Bonn's ECTS categories in this research. Credit attribution, degree equivalence, thesis quality, accepted language evidence, and committee admission stay `unknown` pending document and committee review.

### Ranking is separate from minimum eligibility

- There is **no fixed GPA minimum listed as a formal prerequisite** on the current page. GPA is one selection factor. Ranking for Computer Science uses bachelor's GPA (weight 1); good grades in additional advanced algorithms/complexity modules (2); graphics/vision/audio/sensing modules (2); AI modules (1); additional advanced mathematics (2); academic manuscripts (1); and subject-related teaching experience (1). Only grades better than 2.5 in the German system score points, and only modules beyond the minimum prerequisites count for grade/module points. Applicants should submit their original institution's grade/credit system; Bonn says not to convert foreign grades themselves.
- The page reports that among eligible non-EEA Computer Science applicants for winter 2026/27, roughly the top 1% were admitted; admitted candidates had extra advanced relevant modules and very good grades. The reported threshold for the non-EEA Computer Science selection round was **46 points**. These are intake/competition observations, not durable eligibility thresholds or a guarantee for another semester. Do not use 46 points as a Summer 2027 floor.
- The Examination Board evaluates eligibility and ranking, then eligible applicants are ranked separately by programme and group. Meeting the formal criteria is not a seat offer. An admission offer is valid only for the semester applied for and may be conditional.

### Application evidence and intake timing

Bonn's Institute page says the application is online; all required documents must be uploaded by the applicable deadline. Applicants use the grade and credit system shown in their own university transcript. For Computer Science, winter and summer admission are available. As of this check, Bonn marked winter 2026/27 Computer Science applications closed. For **summer 2027**, the page states a first application period of **1 October–1 November 2026** and a second period **15 December 2026–15 January 2027 for EEA students only**. It defines EEA students for this purpose to include EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway citizens, qualifying applicants completing a bachelor's or master's degree at an EEA university by 31 March 2027, and some family members. The applicable group is not reducible to citizenship alone; check the current instructions for an individual.

Documents listed by Bonn include:

- degree certificates for completed bachelor's/master's degrees; provisional degree certificate if all requirements are fulfilled but the final certificate is pending; or a prescribed examination-office expected-completion confirmation for a near-finished degree. Bonn says an expected-completion confirmation is for remaining exams (including thesis) totalling no more than half a full-time study year, with opportunity to complete them before semester start;
- up-to-date transcripts from all degree programmes, including passed and failed exams for an unfinished German master's;
- APS certificate if previous education was completed in China, India or Vietnam;
- diploma supplement/credit-system explanation and module descriptions or handbook are strongly recommended so the committee can classify foreign credits;
- CV with relevant education/work/other activity from high school onward and countries lived in for over half a year in the previous five years;
- bachelor's thesis in original language, with a detailed proposal/incomplete work allowed in the stated circumstances;
- accepted English evidence; the medium-of-instruction proof alone is explicitly rejected;
- online motivation text of at most 4,000 characters.

Additional subject-specific support (thesis examiner's assessment, publications, employment/teaching evidence, extracurricular work) can help selection but is not a formal minimum requirement for Computer Science. Translations must meet Bonn's language rules; documents outside its listed languages need the original plus a certified translator's or university translation into English, French or German.

## Suggested narrow implementation shape

The source metadata is exported in `src/lib/data/germany-academic-programmes.ts` as the frozen, partial `bonn-computer-science-msc` record. It is descriptive source data only; it does not alter or promote the applied catalog batch. Its four credit thresholds are 18/14/18/12 ECTS and its degree, thesis, accepted English evidence and competitive offer checks explicitly remain manual.

1. Treat this as a separate `programmeAdmission` checklist tied to the exact institution, programme and intake (Bonn · MSc Computer Science · Summer 2027); do not let a generic study-admission fact mark it met.
2. Record applicant-provided evidence separately from committee-reviewed evidence: recognized bachelor level, related-subject decision, each of the three distinct ECTS buckets, thesis size/quality, C1 evidence, application document completeness, applicable applicant group and deadline.
3. Run only mechanical bounds after trusted, categorized evidence is supplied. Foreign equivalence, course relevance, ECTS conversions, thesis evaluation, C1 proof exceptions, group assignment and selection score remain manual/unknown until Bonn's decision. Missing values remain unknown; never default missing credits, proof, or grades to zero or met.
4. Keep formal eligibility, competitive ranking, admission offer, visa, and subsequent residence status as separate states. A positive academic checklist is not an admission offer or student-visa result.
5. Refresh deadlines and current regulations per intake. The 2026/27 ranking statistic and 46-point threshold must be stored as historical selection context only.

## Retrieval limitations

The official Bonn pages were retrieved directly on 2026-10-26. The current official regulations index lists the PO 2023 consolidated republication dated 13 July 2026. The linked bonndoc repository page returned an access-denied response to this retrieval session, so the regulation text was not independently read or cross-checked line by line. The programme's English detailed admission page was available and supplies the exact credit and document rules summarized above; the page points applicants to the German regulation as legally binding. The university may change the webpage, selection process, accepted certificates, document requirements or semester deadlines. Recheck the official pages and obtain committee confirmation before using a real applicant's transcript or application outcome.
