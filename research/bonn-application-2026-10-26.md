The current English deadline-page content lists these Computer Science application windows for summer 2027:

| Second | 15 December 2026–15 January 2027 | EEA students only |

The source gives date ranges but no time zone, closing time, or explicit boundary inclusion. This note preserves the calendar dates as published and does not calculate a deadline instant; any comparison is date-only and leaves the start/end cutoff unknown. The application page warns that portal availability may not reflect the deadline applicable to a specific applicant; applicants must check the deadline page for their own situation. Separate applications are required for each programme.

For the summer 2027 second window, the indexed deadline-page content says EEA students include:

- citizens of an EEA member state: the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway;
- students who will have completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree at an EEA university by 31 March 2027; and
- family members of EEA citizens who may also count, with details referred to the application form or §1(2) VergabeVO NRW.

This is not a citizenship-only test. Family-member qualification is underspecified in the retrieved page and must remain unknown/manual unless the official application form or legal provision is reviewed against the person's facts.

The application page separately describes selection-group classification: anyone with a Bachelor's or higher degree from Germany or another EEA member state counts as German for selection. In the absence of additional proof, a person is counted as foreign. Where a person claims German/EEA selection treatment without a Germany/EEA degree, the page lists possible proof routes: EEA citizenship; Abitur or European Baccalaureate; or German residence plus an EEA-citizen parent who has worked in Germany, or a German-resident EEA-citizen family member who works in Germany. These are selection-group statements and should not be collapsed into a general legal nationality status or treated by themselves as proof of eligibility for the second window.

The page says the committee first assigns the applicant group and checks the deadline for that group, then evaluates requirements and ranks eligible applicants. EEA group, legal-family facts, group-specific deadline, programme eligibility, ranking, and admission remain manual decisions.

## Degree completion evidence

- **Completed degree:** upload degree certificates for every completed Bachelor's and Master's programme.
- **Almost completed:** upload official proof of the expected completion date. It must come from the examination office or higher authority responsible for deciding programme completion. It must certify that remaining examinations (including the thesis) amount to no more than half a year of full-time study and can all be taken before the upcoming semester begins, which the page identifies as 31 March or 30 September. The university does not require an assessment of the likelihood of passing. If the thesis credits are not yet on the transcript, this proof should state the credits expected for the thesis.
- **Completed but certificate not issued:** upload a provisional certificate from the responsible examination office or higher authority explicitly stating that all requirements for the degree are fulfilled. An advisor's thesis-defense letter and a transcript showing credit totals alone are insufficient.
- The application form's expected completion date is the date of the final examination, usually thesis submission or final presentation, rather than the later date a degree certificate is awarded.
- Conditional admission before completion may be possible at the Examination Board's discretion. It does not extend the date by which requirements must be fulfilled: the applicant must satisfy requirements before semester start and prove this within four months after the semester starts, for example with a final or provisional degree certificate showing a pre-semester graduation date. Failure to provide proof leads to expulsion. This cannot be decided by a self-report checklist.

## Implementation boundaries and gaps

- Store rules as dated source metadata. Applicant-entered dates, group, uploads, checkboxes, or document names remain self-reported and unverified; they must not produce a university-confirmed completeness status or admission result.
- Keep application-window membership separate from citizenship and from German immigration status. The family-member and non-degree EEA routes require manual review. Where the user has not supplied enough information, the group and applicable deadline remain unknown.
- Keep “required,” “conditional,” “recommended,” and “optional” distinct. The English-proof branch depends on where both secondary and bachelor's education occurred; European-education evidence is recommended and evaluated by Bonn, not automatically accepted.
- The deadline page could not be opened directly in this retrieval; its official indexed content was available and is recorded with that limitation. No closing time, time zone, or explicit endpoint inclusion was found. Recheck directly and in the portal before implementation or use for a real application.
- This review does not evaluate transcript authenticity, translations, APS authenticity, academic equivalence, module classification, language proof acceptance, conditional admission, completeness as adjudicated by Bonn, selection ranking, or admission offer.
- Linked legal provision (§1(2) VergabeVO NRW) and the live application form were not separately reviewed. Do not infer family-member eligibility details from the page's short reference.
